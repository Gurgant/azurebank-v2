# The American forms that infra/check_british_spelling.py looks for, and the British form of each.
#
# A line is "american -> british". Under a line "endings: ...", each pair is a stem, and the
# check reads the stem with each of those endings; "-" is the stem with no ending. A pair of
# several words is a phrase, read on one line.
#
# A word that is not here is not looked for. That is on purpose: "size", "prize", "seize" and
# "capsize" end like "organize" and are right as they are, so no rule guesses from an ending.
# To hold one more word, add its line.
#
# Left out on purpose, because British writing has both forms or keeps the American one in
# computing: program, disk, dialog (the window), meter (the device), focused, learned, toward,
# inquiry. "Judgment" is listed because this repository writes "judgement".
